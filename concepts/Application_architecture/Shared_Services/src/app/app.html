Why This Pattern is So Clean:

  • Zero Math in Components: Neither NavbarComponent nor BookingPageComponent knows that a ticket costs ₹500 or that GST is 18%. All pricing formulas live inside TicketBookingService.
  • Automatic Reactive Updates: Clicking + or - updates countSignal, which automatically recalibrates subtotal(), gstAmount(), finalTotal(), and the badge in NavbarComponent simultaneously!
